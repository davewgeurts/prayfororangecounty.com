"Silent Rock (also known as the "Silent Curve") in Big Cottonwood Canyon, Utah, is located roughly 2.8 to 3 miles up the canyon near the Storm Mountain Picnic Area.

  Key Details

  • Location: Big Cottonwood Canyon (State Route 190), near the Storm Mountain Day Use / Picnic Area and a small roadside bridge/reservoir section.
  • Distance up canyon: About 2.8 to 3 miles east of the mouth of the canyon (off Wasatch Boulevard).
  • The Tradition: Drivers and passengers traditionally turn off their music and remain completely quiet ("Silent Curve") while passing through this specific rock cut and bend
  to avoid bad luck or accidents.
  • Climbing Reference: Local rock climbing guides (such as around Reservoir Ridge and Dogwood Crag) reference driving about 3 miles up past Silent Rock before parking near the
  bridge.
