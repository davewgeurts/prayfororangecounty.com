# 🌊 Pray for the Ocean (`prayfortheocean.com` / `prayfororangecounty.com`)

Interactive Orange County Surf & Boogie Boarding Pilgrimage:
- **Start**: **Surf & Paddle** in Laguna Beach right next to **The Taco Stand** (699 S Coast Hwy)
- **Pick Your Craft**: Surfboards (Lost Mayhem, Channel Islands, Catch Surf) or Boogie Boards (Morey Mach 7-7, Mike Stewart Science, Catch Surf Womper)
- **Pick Your Pro**: Griffin Colapinto, Caroline Marks, Kelly Slater, Filipe Toledo, Kolohe Andino, Kalani Robb, Mike Stewart, Blair Conklin, Matt "Mayhem" Biolos, and local Super73 E-Bike Groms
- **Cruise Southbound on PCH**: Pass **Treasure Island by the Montage**, **1000 Steps Beach**, and **Salt Creek**
- **Paddle Out at 3 Legendary Spots**: **Dana Point**, **San Clemente Pier**, or **Lower Trestles (WSL Championship Finals)**
- **Living Ocean & Beach**: Cheering fans, sunbathers, kids playing in the surf, lifeguards, seashells, pelicans, sea turtles, dolphins, local enforcers, and WSL Finals heat scoring!
