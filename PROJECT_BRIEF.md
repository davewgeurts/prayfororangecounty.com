# 🌊 prayfortheocean.com / prayfororangecounty.com — Project Brief

## 🎯 Overview & Vision
- **Domains**: `prayfortheocean.com` & `prayfororangecounty.com`
- **Repository**: `https://github.com/davewgeurts/prayfororangecounty.com.git`
- **Concept**: Interactive Orange County coastal surf & boogie boarding pilgrimage from **Laguna Surf & Paddle** (right next to **The Taco Stand** in Laguna Beach) southbound down **Pacific Coast Highway (PCH / CA-1)** past **Treasure Island by the Montage** and **1000 Steps Beach**, arriving at **Dana Point**, **San Clemente Pier**, or **Lower Trestles (WSL Championship Finals)**.
- **Creator**: Dave Geurts (`@davewgeurts`)

## 🏄 Experience Phases
1. **Surf & Paddle Laguna Beach (699 S Coast Hwy — Next to The Taco Stand)**:
   - Pick your **Surfboard** (Lost RNF '96, Lost Driver 3.0, Channel Islands CI Pro, Catch Surf Odysea Log, Surf & Paddle Twin-Pin) OR **Boogie Board / Bodyboard** (Morey Mach 7-7, Mike Stewart Science Pipe, Catch Surf Womper).
   - Pick your **Pro Surfer / Riding Buddy** riding shotgun (Griffin Colapinto, Caroline Marks, Kolohe Andino, Kelly Slater, Filipe Toledo, Stephanie Gilmore, Kalani Robb, Mike Stewart, Blair Conklin, Matt "Mayhem" Biolos, or the Super73 E-Bike Grom Squad).
   - **Sticky Bumps / Fu Wax / Sex Wax Bench Mini-Game**: Basecoat + tropical/cool topcoat, wax comb grooves, and FCS II fin install.
   - **State Beach & WSL VIP Pass Desk**: Golden Poppy Pass, San Clemente Pier Pass, Dana Point Doheny Pass, and WSL Trestles Finals VIP Pass.
2. **Coastal Stops**:
   - **The Taco Stand (Laguna Beach)**: Al Pastor off the spit, California Burrito, Baja Fish Tacos, Churros & Horchata.
   - **Laguna Main Beach Wyland Whaling Wall & Dolphin Shrine**: Circle the coastal loop, honk horn, offer SSW Hurricane Swell prayers.
   - **PCH Coastal 76 / 7-Eleven Surf Fuel & Ice Chest**: Pump gas, grab Zinc & Açaí Bowls, or Gas & Dash down PCH with CHP / Harbor Patrol pursuit.
   - **KROQ 106.7 FM / Surfline Swell Studio**: Vote on "Kook of the Day", play "Beat the Forecaster" OC surf trivia, and claim a free WSL VIP Pass.
3. **PCH Coastal Drive (GTA Top-Down & Windshield Cockpit Views)**:
   - **Mile 0.0**: Surf & Paddle Laguna Beach + The Taco Stand
   - **Mile 1.8**: Main Beach Boardwalk & Lifeguard Tower
   - **Mile 3.2**: Treasure Island Beach by Montage Laguna Beach
   - **Mile 4.5**: 1000 Steps Beach (Sacred Swell Overlook — check the SSW corduroy lines & offer an Ocean Prayer)
   - **Mile 7.2**: Salt Creek & Monarch Bay Headlands
   - **Mile 9.5**: Dana Point Harbor & Doheny State Beach ("Killer Dana")
   - **Mile 13.5**: San Clemente Pier & T-Street Reef
   - **Mile 16.5**: Lower Trestles (San Onofre State Beach — WSL Finals)
4. **Beach Overlook & Surfing / Boogie Boarding Game**:
   - Switch freely between **Dana Point**, **San Clemente Pier**, and **Lower Trestles (WSL Finals)**.
   - Switch between **Surfing** and **Boogie Boarding**.
   - **Living Beach & Ocean**: Cheering WSL fans on the sand, sunbathers under striped umbrellas, kids playing in the shallow ocean & building sandcastles, seashells & starfish to collect, California Lifeguard towers, pelicans & seagulls, sea turtles (Honu), leaping dolphin pods, salty local enforcers in the lineup, and live WSL Finals judge scores at Trestles!
